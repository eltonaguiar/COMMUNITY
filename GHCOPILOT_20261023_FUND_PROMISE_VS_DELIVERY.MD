# Mutual funds: promise versus delivery

Research snapshot: October 23, 2026 UTC. Goal #1. No orders or strategy certification.

## Executive conclusion

Separate four questions: does the fund follow its stated mandate, are advertised historical returns reproducible, did it add value against an appropriate benchmark after expenses, and can this investor actually buy the exact share class?

For a Canadian mutual-fund shortlist, **TDB902 merits practical diligence as an index implementation**, not as a verified best risk-adjusted fund. **RBF460 delivers a balanced allocation but its high Series A fee requires justification**. For active-manager research, **VPMCX and FCNTX have independently observed long-run outperformance**, while **FOCPX has strong issuer-reported benchmark-relative performance requiring independent replication**. None establishes reliable future outperformance.

## PRIMECAP: how a strong story changes with the endpoint

Its official document shows $10,000 growing to $41,039 versus the index's $39,827 from December 31, 2015 to December 31, 2025. These imply approximately **15.165% versus 14.820% annualized**, only **0.345 percentage points/year** apart. It beat the index in **five of those ten calendar years**.

The same document's September 30, 2026 trailing ten-year figures show a larger **1.57pp/year** lead. These are different windows, not a contradiction. This is why one attractive ending date is insufficient evidence of persistent manager skill.

Independent H-141 data on the 2016-2025 calendar window produces approximately 15.098% CAGR for VPMCX. Individual issuer-versus-vendor calendar-year differences reach **0.290pp in 2025** (issuer 29.89%, vendor approximately 29.60%). No adjustment was made to force agreement; distribution/reinvestment conventions and vendor accuracy remain to reconcile. The discrepancy is small relative to the total return but important relative to a narrow claimed excess.

Its September issuer sheet also shows **44.4% in the top ten holdings** and **39.1% technology / 21.5% health care**. These help explain both differentiation and concentration risk.

## ELI5

An index fund is a bus promising to follow a route. Judge whether it follows that route cheaply, not whether it outruns every sports car.

An active fund charges for a driver who chooses shortcuts. Judge whether it arrives sooner than a comparable vehicle after paying the driver, how often the shortcut fails, and how rough the ride gets. PRIMECAP and Contrafund have real historical evidence of arriving ahead; neither promises a smooth ride or guarantees the next shortcut.

A balanced fund promises a mixed load, not an unbreakable vehicle. A low-to-medium risk label is not a guarantee against losses.

## What remains before calling an active fund a durable winner

1. Reconcile adjusted NAV with issuer distribution-reinvestment returns on exact dates and share class.
2. Compare rolling five-/ten-year net results with declared and style-matched benchmarks, not SPY alone.
3. Measure daily loss, recovery time, downside capture and manager-tenure changes.
4. Correct for the complete selection/testing family and survivorship; do not turn an issuer return table into alpha certification.
5. Verify Canadian availability, exact series, loads, advice/platform fees and tax/account fit.
