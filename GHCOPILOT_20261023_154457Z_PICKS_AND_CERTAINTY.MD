## Readiness and certainty definitions

- **CONSIDER AFTER SUITABILITY/ACCESS CHECK:** established exposure and understandable costs; not a timing signal.
- **PROSPECT / DILIGENCE:** interesting historical evidence, but essential validation or access gaps remain.
- **PAPER ONLY:** testable strategy lead, not authorized for real-money deployment.
- **ABSTAIN:** no supported current selection.
- **BUY NOW:** would require current executable evidence and suitability; none assigned.

Certainty describes confidence in a specific evidence statement, **not a probability of profit**. High = strong primary or independently reproduced support for that statement; moderate = partial/source-reported support; low = unresolved independent verification.

These descriptive readiness labels do not replace registry strategy verdicts. No change to strategy gates, positions or approved capital.
