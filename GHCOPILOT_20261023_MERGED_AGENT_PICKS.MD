## Contradictions that matter

1. **OpenCode main versus live versus retraction:** main still says high_trust “PROVEN”; live leads luxalgo CANDIDATE while saying no strategy is proven; BTC retraction exists only in the inspected alternate branch. A reader can get incompatible messages depending on the link.
2. **Live asset classification:** OpenCode's live table labels an AAPL row as CRYPTO. That visible inconsistency is another reason not to treat page ranks as clean per-class evidence.
3. **“Fees explain the entire difference” is not established.** Buffy calls a roughly0.14pp FZROX/VTSAX gap exactly the fee difference. Different total-market indices/sampling, dates and tracking can matter; a matched comparison still does not isolate fees causally. Existing NAV normally already embeds expenses: subtracting a modern expense ratio again can double-charge it.
4. **“Tactical rotation is validated” is disputed.** Buffy says robust; Kilo's older note says verified; the operator's newer review reports conflicting run metrics and suspect SPY drawdown. Require the exact output, actual coverage, daily MTM and all gates before choosing which narrative is true. Lower drawdown or beating a 60/40 comparator does not automatically mean beating SPY.
5. **Inception/proxy honesty:** FZROX/FZILX began in2018. They have no actual2008 history. VTI/VXUS cannot both provide actual2007 histories either: VXUS began in2011. Any earlier proxy must be explicitly identified with its own data, currency and limitations.
6. **A backtest CANDIDATE is not a strict PASS.** n18/42/55 or PF1.44 already misses individual gates; nice analogies cannot change the threshold.

## ELI5: the logic I used to merge the results

First check whether the report is the right edition and whether the logbook is real. Then compare drivers on the same road, with fuel/tolls, measuring crashes during the trip rather than just the finish photo. Finally, test whether a hiring rule chooses good drivers on new trips. Five agents quoting the same finish photo do not create five independent tests.

Technically: source/branch/date provenance; artifact and first-touch checks; same-window adjusted returns; funded daily MTM; costs and concentration; fixed trial history; matching random controls; uncertainty; sealed forward evidence. Missing checks stay unverified. Different gate definitions are not silently merged.
