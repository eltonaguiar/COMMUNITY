## Why these questions lead to the master plan

1. **Fix evidence generation**, not just a graph or a win-rate threshold.
2. **Expose complete supply/selection denominators**, not just finalists.
3. **Evaluate funded portfolios and honest alternatives**, not summed returns.
4. **Freeze a small number of economically distinct experiments**, not another
   strategy factory searching the same already-inspected history.
5. **Let independent future observations decide**, with explicit abstention
   when data or power is insufficient.

The highest-priority new question is now: **which hypothesis can acquire the
most trustworthy *independent economic evidence* per unit of time and risk?**
That is not necessarily the hypothesis with the highest historical PF.
