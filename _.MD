## Common verdict

**No reviewed candidate is independently proven money-ready.** This does not
mean every strategy is worthless, every result fabricated, or every DB table
corrupt. Several headline calculations reproduce; their interpretation fails
because observation timing, executable prices, independence, costs, selection
or portfolio accounting remain unverified.

Use these verdicts consistently: **unverifiable**, **invalid outcome evidence**,
**historical candidate**, **locked prospective experiment**, **confirmed
prospective edge under the stated protocol**. Never use "PROVEN" in an old
paragraph while the top of the same document says downgraded.

## Shared falsification protocol before any promotion

1. **Identity:** freeze strategy/source/symbol/class/version, selection family,
   signal and actual first-observation time, all gates and intended sizing.
2. **Truth:** independent executable market path, correct sessions and units,
   no sentinel/missing/zero substitution, correct expiry and first-touch logic.
3. **Economics:** net full portfolio including adverse fills, spread, fees,
   carry/funding/borrow/roll/distributions and fixed capital/exposure constraints.
4. **Nulls:** compare to tradable matched direction/timing/exposure controls and
   a class-specific benchmark. WR>50% is neither necessary nor sufficient.
5. **Inference:** dependence-aware blocks and confidence intervals; report
   effective independent periods, tails, concentrations, regimes and stress.
6. **Selection:** log every trial, not just finalists; holdout embargo/purging,
   controlled familywise confirmation and no repeated peeking.
7. **Forward:** precommitted sample/date endpoint and power target, independently
   replayed paper book, visible unresolved/rejected observations, no resets.
8. **Decision:** if provenance fails, mark unverifiable; if net tests fail,
   reject that hypothesis; if power/coverage fails, wait. A forced winner per
   asset class is not a valid output.

Do not compute DSR/PBO/SPA from a single selected certificate. These require
the actual trial history and/or aligned return/loss matrices. Missing inputs
mean **not evaluable**, not "PASS".
